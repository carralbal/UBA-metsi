# METSI premium magazine QA rubric

## Blocking checks

- [ ] The source passes `content-depth-standard.md`: no depth dimension below 3 and at least 35/40 overall.
- [ ] The requested minimum word count is met by substantive conceptual body after declared exclusions.
- [ ] Every major concept includes definition, distinction, mechanism, evidence, example, and boundary or decision consequence.
- [ ] The text progresses from an accessible opening to technical and academic depth for the target audience.
- [ ] Recent literature is used in the argument, and the N-document states a unique advance relative to neighboring readings.
- [ ] Eligible source and rendered artifact have equal block count, normalized text, and word count after declared exclusions.
- [ ] Every factual addition is supported or marked as editorial/example material.
- [ ] A page-and-spread plan exists and names layout family, dominant mass, image role, accent, and source IDs.
- [ ] A 16+ page N-document uses at least five layout families; none repeats more than twice consecutively.
- [ ] The spread is balanced: dominant, secondary, and support masses are intentional.
- [ ] Display serif, reading serif, and editorial sans have distinct stable roles.
- [ ] Body size and leading are consistent across narrative pages.
- [ ] Photographs are rectangular, grid-locked, and assigned a documented editorial role.
- [ ] No ordinary photo has rounded corners, shadow, arbitrary centered width, watermark, or visible provider credit.
- [ ] The image set is cohesive and the image registry reports no prohibited repetition.
- [ ] Intentional visual pauses recur every four to six pages; accidental underfill does not masquerade as breathing space.
- [ ] No ordinary page contains more than 40% avoidable empty area.
- [ ] No heading is isolated; no paragraph, list item, caption, label, or diagram text is clipped or awkwardly split.
- [ ] Most infographics occupy one quarter to one half page; any larger feature has a recorded complexity reason.
- [ ] Diagram topology follows semantics and repeated four-box grids or repeated isometric scenes are absent.
- [ ] A document with four or more diagrams uses at least three topologies unless the source report justifies fewer.
- [ ] Every diagram label is grammatical, source-grounded, collision-free, and legible at final PDF size.
- [ ] Hotel Horizonte and burned-matches assets preserve their approved treatment when applicable.
- [ ] The matches close is image-only and full bleed.
- [ ] Previously approved invariants were unchanged or each change has an explicit user-requested reason.
- [ ] References are preserved and clickable where possible.
- [ ] The output regenerates from editable source files without manual PDF patching.

## Visual score, 0–2 each

1. Publication coherence: the document reads as one premium editorial system.
2. Spread composition: density and image mass balance across facing pages.
3. Typographic hierarchy: cover, section, article, body, caption, label, and folio are immediately distinct.
4. Layout variety: composition changes with content without feeling like unrelated templates.
5. Photography: premium, coherent, and semantically relevant.
6. Infographic quality: topology, hierarchy, labels, and scale are content-driven.
7. Rhythm: dense reading, mixed pages, and visual pauses form a deliberate sequence.
8. Content integrity and accessibility: complete source, alt text, contrast, and meaningful links.

Target 15/16 with no blocking failure for a final N-document. A design pilot may be delivered at 14/16 only if its remaining non-blocking deviation is documented.
