# Premium magazine system

## Source

Derived from the four multi-page editorial references supplied by the course owner. The complete page-by-page audit is preserved in the N01 working artifact. This reference contains the reusable rules.

## Governing idea

Consistency does not mean repeating one template. Keep grid, typography, alignment, color discipline, folios, rules, and photographic direction stable while rotating composition families.

## Page and spread geometry

- Print size: A4 portrait, 210 × 297 mm.
- Page grid: six columns; spread grid: twelve columns.
- Safe text margins: 15–19 mm outside, 17–21 mm top, 17–20 mm bottom. Permit image bleed to 0 mm.
- Column gutter: 4–5.5 mm.
- Baseline: 4–4.5 mm.
- Rules: 0.25–0.5 pt.
- Running matter: folio at outside lower corner; author/course line at the opposite lower corner when requested.

## Typography

Use three coordinated roles:

1. Display serif: Didot/Bodoni/Cormorant-style face for cover, major titles, pull quotes, numbers, and drop caps.
2. Reading serif: Baskerville/Source Serif/Georgia-style face for paragraphs and long-form captions.
3. Editorial sans: Avenir/Inter/Helvetica-style face for kicker, folio, labels, tables, metadata, navigation, and compact diagram copy.

Recommended A4 sizes:

- cover masthead: 42–72 pt;
- cover/article title: 27–44 pt;
- section title: 22–32 pt;
- deck: 12–16 pt;
- body: 10.4–11.4 pt at 1.42–1.55 line-height;
- compact module: 8.5–9.8 pt;
- caption/folio: 7.2–8.5 pt;
- drop cap: four to five body lines.

Do not vary body size inside the same narrative stream. Apparent variety must come from role, column width, weight, case, or serif/sans contrast.

## Language and address

- Use Argentine River Plate Spanish in every student-facing N document.
- When a prompt or instruction addresses the reader as `vos`, preserve voseo throughout and use the corresponding imperative: `situá`, `mostrá`, `convertí`, `seleccioná`, `compará`.
- When an index entry or title describes the operation performed by a Núcleo, use third person: `sitúa`, `muestra`, `convierte`, `selecciona`. The grammatical subject is the Núcleo, not the student.
- Do not mix direct instruction and structural description or force declarative and interrogative titles into imperative form.

## Color

- Base paper: `#F7F6F2` or white.
- Primary ink: `#191919`.
- Secondary ink: `#575754`.
- Cool field: `#E2E5E6`.
- Warm stone: `#D8D4CC`.
- Dark field: `#292927`.
- Accent: one restrained coral, amber, cyan, blue-gray, sand, or rust per section.

Concentrate saturated color in one editorial event: a column, one hero image, a mosaic, or a full-page pause. Do not scatter unrelated accents.

## Photography

- Work in coherent series, not isolated stock photos.
- Every METSI N-document cover photograph must originate as a black-and-white photograph. Art direction, light, wardrobe, materials, exposure, contrast, and tonal separation are planned for monochrome before capture or generation. A color photograph later desaturated, recolored, or converted through CSS does not meet the cover standard.
- Assign one role to every photograph: hero, portrait, contextual evidence, sequence, comparison, texture, or pause.
- Use rectangular, grid-locked crops with square corners.
- Use one dominant image per spread; subordinate secondary images through smaller scale or monochrome treatment.
- Allow full bleed, half-page, vertical rail, panorama, collage, or small inset.
- A full-page photograph or dark background must cover the complete A4 canvas to all four edges. White gutters, inherited margins, footer bands, and reduced background rectangles are defects.
- Full bleed is verified on the final PDF, not inferred from HTML or CSS. Rasterize the page and compare the visible artwork with the media box on all four edges. A continuous paper-colored band is a blocking defect. Any browser-scaling compensation must cover every N document in the generation range and must not alter interior pages.
- A stand-alone editorial photograph in an N document is a photographic pause and therefore occupies one complete page to all four edges. Inset photography is reserved for portraits, documentary evidence, instructional details, or images that are semantically part of the surrounding argument.
- Never default every image to the same full-width panoramic band.
- Do not overlay text unless the crop contains a deliberate quiet zone with verified contrast.

## Spread families

### Editorial opener

Folio/section number, kicker, short title, deck, and two or three columns. May pair with a full-page image.

### Article with rail

Two or three text columns plus a narrow rail for images, evidence fragments, quotations, or metadata.

### Framed note

Thin-rule frame containing a definition, editorial letter, worked example, or methodological test. Use once per section at most.

### Mosaic essay

Three to six images aligned to a modular grid, followed or preceded by a concise analytical block. Maintain consistent gaps and one dominant frame.

### Portrait profile

One tall portrait as central mass; text on one side and a smaller image rail or fact panel on the other.

### Panorama bottom

Text occupies the upper half; a grid-aligned panorama anchors the lower half.

### Hero page/spread

Image covers most or all of the page/spread with only a short title, quotation, or caption. Use as deliberate breathing space.

### Accent column

A real grid column in the section accent color containing a pull quote, definition, example, or short argument. Never use as empty decoration.

### Diagram feature

Place a compact infographic in one to three columns or up to half a page. Surround it with article text rather than isolating it as a slide. Full-page diagrams require a documented complexity exception.

## Rhythm contract

- Plan spreads before pages.
- Alternate dense article, mixed article/image, mosaic or rail, and visual pause.
- Every complete N-document includes exactly two internal full-page photographic pauses with one brief, source-grounded reflective phrase each. The first follows the canonical opening/front matter (after page 4); the second marks a later major conceptual transition.
- The two internal pauses never replace the final page. The final page is always the canonical burned-matches image, full bleed and without the large reflective quote used by those pauses. It remains inside the composition system with the standard folio and footer rule, explicit alternative text, and one brief conceptual caption. Do not add a credit or substitute another image.
- Use at least five layout families in 16+ pages.
- Avoid more than two consecutive text-dominant pages.
- Avoid more than two consecutive pages from the same family.
- Include one intentional pause every four to six pages.
- A valid pause is compositionally full; accidental underfill is a defect.

Exercise writing space is not a visual pause. Render it as white or warm-white paper with a thin neutral border, a restrained volt rule, and a clear label. Saturated fills, debug colors, and decorative gradients are defects.

## Regression contract

Before a revision, record approved elements as locked invariants: selected image IDs, crop family, page family, typography, palette, footer, case treatment, closing page, and diagram topology. Compare the new contact sheet against the last approved contact sheet. Do not change a locked invariant unless the user requested that specific change.
